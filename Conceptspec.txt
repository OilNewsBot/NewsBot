Newsstream product concept

Newsstream helps readers and traders follow developments across Energy, Tech,
Healthcare and Industrials. A regulatory decision, an earnings revision or a
change in manufacturing capacity can matter to different readers. Readers
choose sectors and channels, inspect the original source and discuss what the
news could mean for the affected companies and instruments.

Readers can browse public publisher channels, publish their own channels and
subscribe to other curators. The directory starts with genuine RSS stories.
The planned shared feed also supports community posts and public X profiles.
X profiles use official embedded timelines. Authorized structured data or
user-supplied text is required for automated summaries.

A story can collect multiple reports, confirmations and corrections. Keep
the original links visible, distinguish reporting from interpretation, and
label AI summaries. Readers should be able to acknowledge alerts and avoid
repeat notifications about the same development.

Channels that publish explicit trading signals may later show hypothetical
performance with their entry, exit and sizing assumptions. The initial demo
focuses on shared channels, reliable news updates and discussion.

See docs/spec.md for product requirements and docs/feature-overhaul.md for
the sequence and acceptance criteria.
