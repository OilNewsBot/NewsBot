Newsstream product concept

Newsstream brings news, discussion and market context together across Energy, Tech,
Healthcare and Industrials. A reader can find channels, inspect the evidence for
a story and follow the people and publishers whose updates they value.

Public RSS, X profiles, Telegram channels and community posts share the same
directory, channel pages and subscription model. Connecting a personal source
does not publish its contents. Owners choose what to share and can distinguish
reported facts, their interpretation and AI-generated summaries.

The product specification lives in docs/spec.md. The channel and demo guide is
docs/feature-overhaul.md.
