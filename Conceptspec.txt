Newsstream product concept

A reader following a market wants to know which developments deserve a closer
look. Newsstream brings publisher reporting, community discussion and private
sources into one workspace. A channel identifies who to read, a topic watch
identifies subjects of interest, and a story follow tracks a developing event.

The signed-in Home briefing highlights what changed since the last displayed
visit. Each card explains why it appeared and links to the underlying reporting.
Readers can inspect revisions, compare accounts of the same development and
choose which material from their private sources to publish.
